# Soria

> Soria is an AI-native financial terminal built for healthcare investors. It aggregates financial data from 125+ public and private sources into continuously updated, analyst-ready financial models and delivers them through a terminal UI, an MCP server, an API, warehouse sync, and a research feed. Customers include bulge-bracket banks, boutique research firms, and large healthcare-focused hedge funds. Backed by Y Combinator.
